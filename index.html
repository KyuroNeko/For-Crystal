Dear Crystal,
For You
A letter that is simple, honest, and real.

I may not be your first, your last, or your only. You’ve loved before, and you might love again, but if you love me now, then that’s what matters to me. You’re not perfect, and neither am I, and maybe the two of us will never be perfect together.

But if you can make me laugh, make me think twice, and remind me that being human means making mistakes, then I’ll hold on to you and give you the most I can. You may not think about me every second of the day, but you still give you a part of yourself that I know I could break, your heart. That’s why I won’t hurt you, I won’t change you, I won’t overanalyze you, and I won’t expect more than what you can give.

I’ll smile when you make me happy, I’ll tell you when you make me mad, and I’ll miss you when you’re not around. The truth is, everyone gets hurt by someone, you just have to find the person worth suffering for. And honestly, I think that person is you, Crystal.

I know we just met, so I’m not trying to make things sound bigger than they are or rush anything. I just wanted to be honest with you. I like talking to you, I like your presence, and I like getting to know you more. Whatever this is, I’m okay with taking it slow and just letting it happen naturally.

I know we’re still at the start and there’s still a lot for us to learn about each other, but that’s not a bad thing to me. I actually like that part, the little talks, the random moments, and just slowly knowing who you are. No pressure, no forcing, no expectations that are too much. I just want to be real with you and let things flow.

If I’m being honest, I just wanted you to know that what I feel is genuine. That’s all.

With love,
Elijah
